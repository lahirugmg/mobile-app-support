Privacy Policy

Chord Journey for iOS and Android · Effective 6 October 2026
The short version: Chord Journey does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. When you use the microphone features, the sound is analysed live on your device and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

This policy explains how the Chord Journey app (“the app”), published by Lahiru Hettiarachchi Gamage (“we”, “us”), handles information. It applies to the app on iPhone and Android and to this website.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other identifier, and it has no sign-in.

2. Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only so it can provide three optional features:

  • Tuner: detects the pitch of the string you pluck.
  • Listen mode: checks whether the chord you strum matches the song and whether your strums land on the beat.
  • Auto-count: counts your chord changes in the one-minute drill.

Audio from the microphone is processed in real time, in memory, on your device. It is never recorded, saved to a file, uploaded or shared with us or anyone else. The microphone is active only while one of these features is switched on, and you can turn access off at any time in your device settings (iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone; Android: Settings › Apps › Chord Journey › Permissions). The rest of the app works without it.

3. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves the following only on your device: stars and levels completed, best drill scores, the days you practised (for your streak) and your settings (for example whether Listen mode is on). This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. You can erase it at any time with Chords › Settings › Reset progress, or by deleting the app.

4. Analytics, advertising and third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no third-party tracking. We do not sell, rent or share information with third parties, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

If you download the app from the Apple App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google may collect information under their own privacy policies (for example, purchase and download records). We do not control that processing.

5. Network use

The app works fully offline. It does not send data to any server operated by us.

6. Contacting support

If you email us for help, we receive the email address and any information you choose to include. We use it only to reply to you, and we delete support emails when they are no longer needed.

7. Children

Chord Journey is suitable for learners of all ages. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data about app users, there is normally nothing for us to access or delete. Data on your device is under your control, as described in section 3. If you have a question about your rights, contact us below.

9. This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and secure the site. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when fonts are downloaded.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
