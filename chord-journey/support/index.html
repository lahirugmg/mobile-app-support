Getting started

How do levels unlock?

Each level has 2–3 missions. Earn at least one star in every mission and the next level unlocks. Already play a little? Turn on Chords › Settings › Unlock all levels.

How are stars awarded?

With Listen mode on, Chord Journey suggests stars from how many chords were right, how many strums were on time, and how much of the song it heard. You can always rate yourself instead. Practising below 100% speed earns up to one star, so play at full speed for three.

What do the strum arrows mean?
  • ↓ down-strum, ↑ up-strum (light, top strings), · rest (keep your hand moving)
  • B / b: pick the bass note, then the alternate bass note
  • ✕: muted “chuck”; T: thumb on the root when fingerpicking

Listen mode & the microphone

Listen mode isn’t hearing me
  • Allow microphone access: iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone › Chord Journey. Android: Settings › Apps › Chord Journey › Permissions › Microphone.
  • Put the phone about an arm’s length from the guitar, with the bottom microphone facing it.
  • Play in a quiet room. Fans, TV and talking can confuse chord detection.
It says I played the wrong chord

Check every string rings clearly (no muted or buzzing strings) and that your guitar is in tune with the built-in Tuner. Close relatives such as Em and Em7 are accepted. If a chord you’re sure is right keeps failing, email us your device model and the chord name.

Why is the backing guitar off when Listen is on?

The microphone would hear the app’s own guitar sound. Wear headphones if you’d like to keep the backing guitar while listening. The metronome click is pitched so the app ignores it.

Timing feedback says I’m early or late

“On the beat” means within ±70 ms. Chord Journey adjusts for your device’s audio delay after a few strums, so give it the first couple of bars before judging.

Is my playing recorded?

No. Audio is analysed live on your device and never recorded, stored or uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

Sound, tuner & progress

I can’t hear the app on iPhone

Turn up the volume and check Silent mode. If there’s still no sound, close and reopen the app. Bluetooth speakers and headphones may add a little delay.

How do I tune my guitar?

Open Tuner, tap Listen and pluck one string at a time until the needle is centred and turns green. You can also tap a string name to hear its reference note.

How do I reset my progress?

Chords › Settings › Reset progress. Progress lives only on your device, so deleting the app also erases it.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Not at the moment. For privacy, progress is stored only on your device.

About the songs
