Privacy Policy

Soccer Skills: Drills & Moves for iPhone · Effective 28 October 2026
The short version: Soccer Skills does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. When you practice with the camera, the picture is shown live on your screen as a mirror and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

This policy explains how the Soccer Skills: Drills & Moves app (“the app”), published by Lahiru Hettiarachchi Gamage (“we”, “us”), handles information. It applies to the app on iPhone and to this website.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other identifier, and it has no sign-in.

2. Camera

The app asks for camera access only for the optional Practice with Camera feature, which shows a live view from your camera so you can watch your form while you practice a drill.

The camera picture is displayed on your screen in real time and nothing else. It is never recorded, saved to a file, analysed, uploaded or shared with us or anyone else. The camera is on only while the practice screen is open, and you can turn access off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera. The rest of the app works without it.

The app never uses the microphone.

3. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves which drills you have marked as mastered, only on your device. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. You can change it at any time by tapping Mastered on a drill, or erase it by deleting the app.

4. Analytics, advertising and third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no third-party tracking. We do not sell, rent or share information with third parties, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

If you download the app from the Apple App Store, Apple may collect information under its own privacy policy (for example, download records). We do not control that processing.

5. Network use

The app works fully offline. It does not send data to any server operated by us.

6. Contacting support

If you email us for help, we receive the email address and any information you choose to include. We use it only to reply to you, and we delete support emails when they are no longer needed.

7. Children

Soccer Skills is suitable for players of all ages. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data about app users, there is normally nothing for us to access or delete. Data on your device is under your control, as described in section 3. If you have a question about your rights, contact us below.

9. This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and secure the site. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when fonts are downloaded.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
