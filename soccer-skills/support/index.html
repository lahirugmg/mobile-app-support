Getting started

How do I find a drill?

Search by name on the home screen, or filter by level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Pro) and by category (Dribbling, Ball Control / First Touch, Footwork, Turns & Moves, Speed & Agility).

How do I read the drill diagram?
  • The blue dot is your left foot, the orange dot your right foot and the white dot the ball. Triangles are cones.
  • Tap play to watch the move, or use the step buttons to go through it one phase at a time.
  • Tap 1x to switch to half speed (0.5x) and back.
How does “Mark as Mastered” work?

On a drill’s page, tap Mark as Mastered once you can do the move comfortably. The home screen shows how many of the 18 drills you’ve mastered. Tap Mastered again to undo it.

Practicing with the camera

How should I set up my phone?

Tap Practice with Camera on any drill. Prop your phone up so your whole body and the ball fit inside the guide on screen, then tap the red button to start a set. Use the switch-camera button to change between the front and back cameras.

The camera screen is black or asks for access
  • Allow camera access: Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera › Soccer Skills. The camera screen also has an Open Settings button if access is turned off.
  • Close other apps that might be using the camera, then reopen the drill.
Is my video recorded?

No. The camera is a live mirror only: nothing is recorded, saved, analysed or uploaded. When you finish a set, you tick the checkpoints you hit yourself. See the Privacy Policy.

Progress

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app erases it.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Not at the moment. For privacy, progress is stored only on your device.

Training safely

Practice on a flat, open space with room around you, warm up first, and stop if anything hurts. Younger players should train with an adult nearby.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 15.1 or later. Works offline.
